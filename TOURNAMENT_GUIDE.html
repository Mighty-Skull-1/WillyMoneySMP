🏆 Willy Money SMP

Official Tournament Guide, Gameplay Mechanics & Administration Manual

📖 Tournament Overview

Willy Money SMP is a high-stakes, team-based Minecraft competitive engine. Players are classified into skill tiers, auctioned in live Captains' draft bidding, build bases during a grace period, battle in custom events, and fight to the death with permadeath elimination in the Finale.

🪙 The Two Currencies

Currency Who Uses It Main Purpose How to Obtain
Draft Credits Team Captains Used strictly during the Draft Phase to bid on tier players in auctions. Given at start (400 default) or set by admins via /credits.
Game Credits All Players Used in the in-game /shop and to revive eliminated teammates. Earned via Kills, winning KOTH, looting Airdrops, and completing Bingo lines.

⏳ Tournament Phases

Phase 1

📋 Draft Phase

PvP Status: Disabled.

Team Captains bid on nominated players during live 30-second auctions using Draft Credits. Won players are automatically added to the winning Captain's team.

/smp phase draft
Phase 2

🛡️ Grace Period

PvP Status: Strictly Blocked.

Friendly fire and player combat are disabled. Teams explore the world, mine resources, craft weapons and armor, and build fortified bases.

/smp phase grace
Phase 3

⚔️ PvP Enabled

PvP Status: Active (Friendly Fire Disabled).

Combat is turned on! Eliminated players drop into Spectator mode upon death. Kills award Game Credits. Captains can buy back teammates for 150 credits.

/smp phase pvp
Phase 4

👑 Finale Showdown

PvP Status: Full Combat.

The endgame battle for tournament victory. Surviving teams converge with shop buffs and remaining members until one team stands alone!

/smp phase finale

☠️ Elimination & Buyback System

When PvP is active (Phase 3 & 4), player deaths are permanent:

  • Spectator Mode: Upon dying, a player is struck with lightning, a global Wither sound echoes, and they are placed into Spectator mode.
  • Kill Credit Rewards:
    • Eliminating a Captain: +25 Game Credits
    • Eliminating a Tier A player: +15 Game Credits
    • Eliminating a Tier B / C player: +10 Game Credits
  • Captain Buyback / Revive:

    Team Captains can revive fallen teammates at any time by running:

    /team revive <player>

    Cost: 150 Game Credits. The revived player is restored to Survival mode and instantly teleports to their Captain's side!

🎯 Live Tournament Events

1. 🏰 King of the Hill (KOTH)

How to Start: /koth start or via /smp Control Panel.

A 5-block particle flame ring appears with a floating 180-second holographic timer. Teams holding the zone without enemies inside score capture points. If rival teams enter, the hill is marked CONTESTED. The team with the most points at 0s wins +50 Game Credits awarded to their Captain!

2. 🪂 Proximity Airdrops

How to Summon: /airdrop random <common|rare|legendary> or click airdrop buttons in /smp.

Supply crates parachute from the sky 50–130m near an active player, leaving a smoke beacon signal on impact. Chests are filled with randomized distinct gear and grant instant credits on claim:

  • Common Drop: Varied food, iron, arrows, shields + trash loot + 8–15 Credits.
  • Rare Drop: Diamonds, golden apples, pearls, bow + 16–25 Credits.
  • Legendary Drop: Netherite scraps, Totem of Undying, God apples, diamonds + 35–50 Credits.

3. 🧩 Lockout Bingo

How to Start: /bingo start. View board with /bingo.

An interactive 5x5 board featuring 25 survival tasks (e.g., Mine Ancient Debris, Tame a Cat, Craft Beacon, Brew Invisibility). When a team completes a task, it turns into that team's wool color and is locked out from everyone else. Completing a 5-in-a-row line awards +150 Game Credits to the team Captain!

🔄 Full Tournament Reset

⚠️ Host Testing Reset Tool

When you finish test sessions, you can completely restore the server to a clean state without reinstalling files:

Command: /smp reset confirm
GUI: Shift + Click the TNT (Slot 25) in /smp.

What it does:

  • Restores all players to Survival mode at world spawn.
  • Fills health and food to maximum, removes potion effects, resets XP to 0.
  • Clears test items from inventories and ender chests.
  • Revives all spectators and clears elimination tracking.
  • Disbands all teams, clears player tiers, and resets credit balances.
  • Sets the tournament phase back to Draft Phase.
  • Wipes data.yml and writes a fresh save.
